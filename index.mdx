Nomo is an iPhone app for people whose work depends on a coding agent they
can't watch all day. It does three things.

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Status" href="/status" icon="signal">
    Is Claude up? Widget plus an alert when a provider goes down. No sign-in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage" href="/usage" icon="gauge">
    How much of your plan is left, with alerts at 70%, 90%, and 100%.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sessions" href="/sessions/overview" icon="mobile-screen">
    Live sessions on your Lock Screen, and permission prompts you can answer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
