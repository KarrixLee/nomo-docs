## Status and usage

Two widgets on your Home Screen: whether a provider is up, and how much of your
plan is left. Both send a notification when something changes — an outage, or a
limit you're about to hit. Free, and nothing to install on your computer.

## Sessions <span className="pro-pill" />

Your agent keeps running while you walk away. Nomo puts the session on your
Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and when it stops to ask permission for
something, you answer from your phone.

Sessions follows the agent on your computer rather than the account behind it,
so the list is shorter — and it needs the plugin installed there.
