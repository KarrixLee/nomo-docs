Claude Code runs on your Mac. You don't have to sit in front of it.

Nomo Sessions puts your running Claude Code sessions on your iPhone — on the
Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. When Claude stops to ask permission for
something, your phone tells you, and you can answer it from there.

Walk to the kitchen. Claude keeps working. It hits a command it needs approval
for, your phone buzzes, you tap **Allow**, and it keeps going. You never opened
the laptop.

<Note>
  Sessions is part of Nomo Pro. Status monitoring and usage widgets are free and
  work without any of the setup below.
</Note>

## What you need

- An iPhone running iOS 18 or later
- Claude Code installed on a Mac
- The two paired together — [that's the next page](/sessions/pair), and it takes about a minute

## How it fits together

Your Mac sends session updates to your phone through Nomo's relay. The relay
can't read them: everything is encrypted on your Mac and only decrypted on your
phone. Nomo's servers move sealed data around without ever holding the key.

That matters for what you can expect — your prompts, file paths, and commands
are yours. It also means pairing is a real cryptographic step, not just a login,
which is why it uses a QR code.
