Most of what looks like a bug here is Nomo being honest about not knowing
something. This page is what each of those states actually means, grouped by
where you'd go to fix it.

If more than one thing looks wrong at once, skip ahead and
[start with status](#start-with-status) — it usually names the problem in one
screen.

## Fix it on your phone

Nothing to do at your Mac for any of these.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Nothing appears on the Dynamic Island" icon="circle-dot">
  Almost always the intended behaviour: sessions are **opt-in**. Open the
  computer, find the session, flip its switch — or turn on **Auto add new
  sessions** in [Settings](/sessions/settings#dynamic-island).

  If the switch is on and still nothing shows, check **Live Activities** is
  enabled for Nomo in iOS Settings. Then check that another session isn't
  holding the island: your phone shows [one at a
  time](/sessions/island#which-session-it-picks), and a session that has been
  waiting on you outranks one that's working.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A session's island switch is on, greyed out, and won't turn off" icon="toggle-off">
  That session is asking permission. A prompt reaches your island whichever way
  its switch is set — otherwise a prompt that arrived while your phone was in
  your pocket would be one you couldn't answer, which is the whole point of the
  feature. So the switch shows **on** while the prompt waits, because that's
  what's actually happening.

  **Why you can't turn it off:** turning it off wouldn't do anything. The prompt
  would stay on the island regardless — that's the only way it can reach you —
  so the switch would flick back on under your thumb a second later. Rather than
  give you a control that ignores you, Nomo holds it still until it can obey you
  again.

  Nothing you chose was overwritten. Answer the prompt and the switch goes
  straight back to off, where you left it — or within the hour, if nobody
  answers. Then it's yours to set again.

  None of this happens with **Remote approvals** off, which is the default: your
  switch is never overridden. See
  [Answering from your phone](/sessions/approvals).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The island is frozen, or says Disconnected?" icon="wifi">
  The island shows the last thing your Mac sent. After ten minutes of silence it
  drains of colour and says `Disconnected?` rather than continuing to imply the
  session is live.

  Your Mac asleep, off the network, or the terminal closed all land here. Open
  the Nomo app to force a refresh — a stale state corrects itself immediately.
  If it doesn't, the Mac genuinely isn't reporting: run the status command
  below.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Prompts reach my phone but there's nothing to tap" icon="hand">
  Check the **colour**, not the icon. Yellow and violet both draw a raised hand.
  Yellow (`Need help`) means the agent stopped for something only the terminal
  can take. Violet (`Needs permission`) is the answerable one.

  If everything is yellow and never violet, **Answer on iPhone** is probably
  off — it's off by default. See
  [Answering from your phone](/sessions/approvals).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Fix it on your computer

Your phone is fine. These are all settled from a session on the Mac.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="The drawer says remote approvals are paused" icon="pause">
  Someone ran the pause command on that computer. Resume it from a session on
  that machine:

  <CodeGroup>
  ```bash Claude Code icon="/images/claude-code.svg"
  /nomo-cc:approvals on
  ```

  ```bash Codex icon="/images/codex.svg"
  $nomo-approvals on
  ```

  ```bash OpenCode icon="/images/opencode.svg"
  /nomo-approvals on
  ```
  </CodeGroup>

  One switch per computer, shared by every agent —
  [more](/sessions/commands#approvals).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Codex stopped sending prompts after an update" icon="code">
  Codex hooks ship **inert until trusted**, and a plugin update that adds a hook
  re-opens that review. Run `/hooks` in Codex and trust the Nomo entries. Until
  you do, approvals fall back to the terminal and nothing reports an error.

  More in [Using Codex](/sessions/codex#hooks-have-to-be-trusted).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Options are missing when I touch and hold" icon="ellipsis">
  **Always allow**, **Deny with note…** and **Answer…** all need plugin
  **2.0.0** — answering from the phone is what 2.0 added. Nomo hides options the
  Mac can't honour rather than offering a button that would do nothing.

  A `Plugin update available` banner at the top of the drawer means exactly
  that. Update from the same command you installed with.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Rows look stale or duplicated" icon="broom">
  Clear the dead ones without unpairing:

  <CodeGroup>
  ```bash Claude Code icon="/images/claude-code.svg"
  /nomo-cc:reset
  ```

  ```bash Codex icon="/images/codex.svg"
  $nomo-reset
  ```

  ```bash OpenCode icon="/images/opencode.svg"
  /nomo-reset
  ```
  </CodeGroup>

  Live sessions, your pairing and your keys are left alone —
  [what it prints](/sessions/commands#reset). An OpenCode row belongs to
  OpenCode's server, so quit OpenCode first if one refuses to clear —
  [why](/sessions/opencode#reset-needs-opencode-closed).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Pairing and decryption

Your phone is receiving updates it can't read, or a computer it no longer has a
key for. All three end at the same fix: pair that computer again.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="A row says Encrypted session, or Reconnecting…" icon="lock">
  Your phone received the update but hasn't unlocked it yet. Nomo retries every
  few seconds, and this usually clears itself within one poll.

  If it persists, your phone no longer holds the right key for that computer —
  the Mac was re-paired, or the pairing was replaced. Pair it again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A row says Unavailable" icon="ban">
  The same thing, after ten minutes of failing. Rather than leaving a row that
  looks alive but isn't, Nomo marks it dead and stops guessing at its contents.
  It won't drive the island and it won't be counted as active. Re-pairing the
  computer restores it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A computer says No longer paired" icon="laptop">
  That pairing was revoked — you re-paired the Mac, or ran
  [`/nomo-cc:unpair`](/sessions/commands#unpair) on it. Nomo waits for a
  sustained run of failures before believing it, so this is never a
  flaky-minute-of-Wi-Fi diagnosis.

  Tap **Remove**, or leave it — it clears itself about a minute later. Pair the
  computer again when you want it back.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Start with status

If more than one thing looks wrong, run
[`/nomo-cc:status`](/sessions/commands#status) on the Mac before anything else.
One screen tells you whether the machine is paired, whether its watchdog is
alive, and whether events are actually reaching your phone — which is usually
enough to say what's broken.

`Last event sent: never` with a session genuinely running means nothing is being
picked up. Restart Claude Code so its hooks load; for Codex, run `/hooks`; for
OpenCode, restart it so the plugin loads.

Every command, and what its output means, is on
[Commands](/sessions/commands).

## Still wrong? Send it over

Turn on **Session diagnostics** in [Settings](/sessions/settings#debug), then
expand the session that's misbehaving. A line of Nomo's own internal state
appears underneath it, something like:

```
2.0.0 · lan:hold 0.4s · dp
```

Don't try to decode it — that's our job. Screenshot the row and send it, along
with what you expected and what you got instead:

  <Card title="Open an issue" href="https://github.com/KarrixLee/nomo/issues" icon="github">
    Best for anything reproducible. Public, so leave out anything private.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" href="mailto:karrixlee1231@gmail.com" icon="envelope">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The output of [`/nomo-cc:status`](/sessions/commands#status) from the affected
computer helps too.

## Nothing's broken — you just want something

"Why can't I do X from my phone?" is often a fair question rather than a bug.
Some of it is a real limit of what an agent hands to a plugin, and that's
written down: [Using Codex](/sessions/codex) and [Using
OpenCode](/sessions/opencode) cover those two agents, and [Answering from your
phone](/sessions/approvals) covers what a Live Activity can and can't host.

The rest is just not built yet.

## Starting over

[`/nomo-cc:unpair`](/sessions/commands#unpair) on the affected computer removes
the pairing and erases its key. Then [pair it again](/setup/pairing).

Forgetting a computer **from the phone** is not the same thing: it stops that
computer appearing in your app, but the Mac keeps its local state until you run
that command there.
