The **Sessions** tab is a list of **computers**, not sessions. One card per Mac
you've paired. Your sessions live one level down — tap a computer to open it.

That surprises people who scan the tab looking for a session they just started.
It's on the card, inside the count.

## The computer list

Each card carries the computer's name, a one-line summary, and a coloured dot
for the connection.

| Dot | Summary line | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | `Active recently` | Something happened on this Mac in the last 10 minutes. |
| Grey | `No recent activity` | Paired and healthy, just quiet. |
| Amber | `Reconnecting…` | Nomo couldn't reach the relay for this computer on the last poll. It retries on its own. |
| Spinner | `Connecting…` | Freshly paired, still waiting for its first session to land. |
| Grey | `No longer paired` | This pairing was replaced or removed. See [troubleshooting](/sessions/troubleshooting). |
| Grey | `Paused — renew to reconnect` | Nomo Pro lapsed. |

The summary reads `No active sessions`, `1 session`, or `3 active · 7 sessions`.
"Active" excludes finished sessions, unreadable ones, and any session that
claims to be working but has been silent for more than 10 minutes.

A chip in the top-right shows how many computers you've paired. It turns orange
if you hit the cap.

<Note>
  Touch and hold a card to **Rename** or **Forget This Computer**. Renaming is
  local to your phone. Forgetting stops the computer from appearing here — you
  can pair it again anytime, and it doesn't touch anything on the Mac itself.
</Note>

## Inside a computer

Tapping a card opens a drawer with a segmented control at the top: **Claude
Code** and **Codex**. Nomo opens whichever agent was active most recently.

Sessions inside are grouped **by project folder**, with the folder's git branch
shown next to the header. One folder gets a single **Sessions** card; two or
more get one card each, named after the folder.

Under the computer name at the top you'll see whether it's `Active`, `Last
active`, or `Reconnecting…` — and, when Nomo is talking to the Mac directly
over your network rather than through the relay, a green **⚡ Local** chip. See
[Local network](/sessions/settings#local-network).

## What a session row shows

<ResponseField name="Status icon" type="left edge">
  A dot, a hand, a checkmark, or a spinner. Colour is the signal — see the table
  below.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Title">
  The agent's own summary of what it's working on, so it changes as the session
  moves between tasks. It is **not** the folder name — though it falls back to
  the folder name when the agent hasn't summarised anything yet.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Status line">
  The state, plus what it's doing right now when the agent reported it —
  `Running · editing`, `Needs permission · Bash`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Relative time">
  Time since the **last event**, not how long the session has been running.
  `2m ago` means Nomo last heard from it two minutes ago. Elapsed run time only
  appears on the [Dynamic Island](/sessions/island).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Toggle" type="right edge">
  Puts this session on the Dynamic Island. Off by default — see
  [Settings](/sessions/settings#dynamic-island).
</ResponseField>

## Session states

| Label | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Running` | Orange dot | Working. Nothing needed from you. |
| `Needs attention` | Orange hand | Stopped and waiting on you, with nothing you can answer from the phone. |
| `Needs permission · Bash` | Violet hand | Asking permission for a specific action. **This one you can answer here** — see [Answering from your phone](/sessions/approvals). |
| `Needs an answer` | Violet hand | Asking a multiple-choice question. Also answerable from the phone. |
| `Done` | Green check | The session finished. |
| `Reconnecting…` | Amber | Either the Mac lost contact with the relay mid-session, or Nomo hasn't been able to decrypt this row yet. Transient — it retries. |
| `Unavailable` | Grey dot | A row Nomo still can't decrypt after 10 minutes. It shows as `Encrypted session`. |
| `Sending…` | Spinner | Your answer is on its way to the Mac. |

Once a prompt has been settled, the row picks up a small caption saying how:
`Allowed`, `Denied`, `Always allowed`, `Answered`, or an honest failure like
`Expired — continue on your computer`. The full list is in
[Answering from your phone](/sessions/approvals#what-the-caption-tells-you).

Rows are ordered by most recent activity. Nomo never hides a row — the list
mirrors what the relay holds, exactly.

## Tap a row for detail

Tapping the text side of a row expands it. Everything here is selectable, and
labelled with a symbol rather than a word:

- **Model** — `Fable`, `Opus 4.8`, `GPT-5`. Hidden when the agent didn't report one.
- **Full title** — untruncated.
- **Folder** and **session ID** — the first eight characters, monospaced.
- **The pending question**, in full, when there is one.
- **View plan** — opens the plan. Answerable for Claude Code, read-only for [Codex](/sessions/codex).
- **Open on Mac** — focuses that session's terminal window on your Mac. Only appears when Nomo has a direct [local network](/sessions/settings#local-network) link.

<Tip>
  To **answer** a prompt, touch and hold the row rather than tapping it. Tapping
  expands; holding opens the Allow / Deny menu.
</Tip>

## How many

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Sessions per computer | **20**, across both agents |
| Paired computers | **5** |
| Sessions on the Dynamic Island | **One at a time**, device-wide |

Both numbers are set on Nomo's side rather than compiled into the app, so they
can move without an app update. The session count shows as an `N/20` chip on
the first Sessions card, and the computer count as an `N/5` chip on the tab.
Both turn orange at the cap.

Pairing a sixth computer fails with **Computer Limit Reached**. Forget one you
no longer use and try again — computers you've already paired are never revoked
to make room.

When a computer is at its session cap, Nomo sheds finished and long-silent rows
first. A genuinely new session is refused rather than pushing a live one off —
unless it arrives holding a permission prompt, which always wins.

## Rows that disappear

Finished and silent sessions retire on their own, roughly an hour after their
last real activity. A row vanishing by itself is normal and means nothing went
wrong.

A pairing that's been revoked — you re-paired that Mac, or ran
`/nomo-cc:unpair` on it — is a different thing. Nomo waits for a sustained
run of failures before believing it, then flips the card to `No longer paired`
with a one-tap **Remove**, and clears it a minute later. It won't silently drop
a computer over a flaky minute of Wi-Fi.
