By default, **no session appears on your Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.** You
pick the ones you want there.

This surprises people, so it's worth being blunt about it: if you paired
successfully and your island is empty, nothing is broken. You haven't opted a
session in yet.

## Why it works this way

The island is prime real estate — it sits on your Lock Screen all day. If every
session you started claimed it, one busy afternoon would bury your phone in
sessions you stopped caring about an hour ago.

So the list in the app shows everything, and the island shows only what you
chose.

## Turning a session on

Open the **Sessions** tab, find the session, and switch it on.

It appears on the island within a few seconds, and stays until you switch it off
or the session ends.

## A reasonable habit

Opt in the one long-running session you're actually waiting on. Leave the rest
in the app.

If you're running several sessions and want to be told about all of them,
notifications cover that without any of them taking the island.

## It survives a restart

Your choices persist. Quitting the app, restarting your phone, or the Mac
briefly dropping off the network doesn't reset them.

If a computer was unreachable when you toggled something, the change is held and
applied once it comes back.
