Nomo doesn't show Grok Bot chats as sessions. Grok Bot isn't in the Sessions
list or on the Dynamic Island. What you get is its status, your usage, and the
days you used it.

## What you get

| | Plan | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grok Bot's status, in the widget and in alerts | Free | Nothing |
| Your usage, in the widget and in alerts | Free | Sign in to Cursor in Nomo |
| The Pace line and the Nomo Pace widget | Nomo Pro | Sign in to Cursor in Nomo |
| Grok Bot days on Nomo Activity | Nomo Pro | A Mac with the Grok Bot app and Nomo |

## On your iPhone only {#iphone-only}

The first three rows need no computer. Each one has its own page:

- **Status**: pick Cursor's **Grok Bot** service in the widget. See
  [Status](/status) and [Widget](/widget).
- **Usage**: sign in to Cursor. See [Cursor and Grok](/usage#cursor-and-grok).
- **Alerts**: see [Notifications](/notifications).
- **Pace**: see [The Pace line](/usage#pace) and [Nomo Pace](/widgets#nomo-pace).

## Count Grok Bot days on your streak {#streak}

These steps are for a Mac where Grok Bot is your only AI app. You need Nomo
2.4.2 and Nomo Pro.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Grok Bot on your Mac">
    Install the Grok Bot desktop app on your Mac, and sign in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install Nomo">
    Open Terminal. It's the Mac app for typing commands. Press Command-Space,
    type `Terminal`, and press Return.

    Paste this and press Return:

    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash bunx icon="terminal"
    bunx nomo-ai --grokbot
    ```

    ```bash npx icon="terminal"
    npx nomo-ai --grokbot
    ```
    </CodeGroup>

    You need bun or Node.js 18 or later for this. See
    [Installation](/setup/install).

    It adds a small background job. The job runs when you log in and once an
    hour, and counts your Grok Bot days. You may see a macOS notice that says
    **Background Items Added**. That's this job.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pair your iPhone">
    Skip this step if this Mac is already paired. Pairing again replaces the
    old pairing.

    ```bash
    bunx nomo-ai pair
    ```

    A page with a QR code opens in your browser. In Nomo, tap the **Sessions**
    tab, then **Pair a Computer**, and scan the code. See
    [Pairing](/setup/pairing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check it works">
    ```bash
    bunx nomo-ai status
    ```

    The **Phone** line should say `paired`. There's no Grok Bot line here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Add the Nomo Activity widget. Touch and hold it, tap **Edit Widget**, tap
    **Character**, and pick **Grok Bot**. See
    [Nomo Activity](/widgets#nomo-activity).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To see your days right away, run `bunx nomo-ai reset` on your Mac. When it
says `Sent your activity summary (heatmap and streak) to your phone.`, open the
[Activity screen](/widgets#activity-screen) in Nomo and pull down.

<Note>
  Already use Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor or Antigravity with Nomo on
  this Mac? You don't need `--grokbot`. Just
  [update the plugin](/sessions/commands#update) to 2.4.2.
</Note>

## Limits

- Without the Grok Bot app on a Mac, there's nothing to count.
- Chats you had on your phone or the web count too, once the Mac app has
  synced them. Open the Mac app now and then.
- The Mac app keeps only recent messages for each Bot. So older days may not
  show up the first time.
- Grok Bot has no token numbers. It adds days and time to Nomo Activity, but
  nothing to Nomo Tokens.
- On The X Subscription, Grok Bot and Cursor share one weekly limit, and Nomo
  shows it. On an older Cursor plan, Grok Bot has its own limit, and Nomo
  doesn't read it.
- Mac only, for now.

## Remove it {#remove}

```bash
bunx nomo-ai uninstall --grokbot
```

This removes only the background job. Your iPhone stays paired. To disconnect
the Mac as well, run unpair **first**. See
[Without slash commands](/sessions/commands#without-slash-commands).

Only counts leave your Mac, and they're end-to-end encrypted. The plugin's
README lists [what leaves your computer](https://github.com/KarrixLee/nomo#what-leaves-your-computer)
and [what the background job does](https://github.com/KarrixLee/nomo#grok-bot-background-job).
