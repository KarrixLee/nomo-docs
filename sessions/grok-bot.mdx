Nomo doesn't show Grok Bot chats as sessions. Grok Bot isn't in the Sessions
list or on the Dynamic Island. What you get is its status, your usage, and the
days and tokens you used. It all works on your iPhone. You don't need a
computer.

## What you get

| | Plan | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grok Bot's status, in the widget and in alerts | Free | Nothing |
| Your usage, in the widget and in alerts | Free | Cursor connected in Nomo |
| The Pace line and the Nomo Pace widget | Nomo Pro | Cursor connected in Nomo |
| Grok Bot days and tokens on Nomo Activity and Nomo Tokens | Nomo Pro | Cursor connected in Nomo |

## On your iPhone {#iphone-only}

Each row has its own page:

- **Status**: pick Cursor's **Grok Bot** service in the widget. See
  [Status](/status) and [Widget](/widget).
- **Usage**: connect Cursor. See [Cursor and Grok](/usage#cursor-and-grok).
- **Alerts**: see [Notifications](/notifications).
- **Pace**: see [The Pace line](/usage#pace) and [Nomo Pace](/widgets#nomo-pace).
- **Days and tokens**: see the next section.

## Count Grok Bot on your streak {#streak}

You need Nomo 2.4.2 and Nomo Pro.

<Warning>
  Connect **Cursor**, not Grok. Connecting **Grok** in Nomo shows your weekly
  limit, but not your Grok Bot days or tokens. Those come only through Cursor.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Cursor">
    1. Open Nomo.
    2. Tap the **Providers** tab.
    3. Tap **Cursor**.
    4. Tap **Usage** at the top.
    5. Tap **Connect Cursor account**.
    6. iOS asks to use **cursor.com** to sign in. Tap **Continue**.
    7. On Cursor's page, sign in with the same account you use for Grok Bot.
       That's your Grok or X account.

    The page closes by itself. Already connected Cursor with that account?
    Skip this step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Activity screen">
    Open the [Activity screen](/widgets#activity-screen). It fetches your Grok
    Bot days and tokens when it opens. You can also pull down to fetch again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Add the Nomo Activity or Nomo Tokens widget. Touch and hold it, tap
    **Edit Widget**, tap **Character**, and pick **Grok Bot**. See
    [Nomo Activity](/widgets#nomo-activity).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What counts

- A day counts when Grok Bot used any tokens that day. So scheduled routines
  count too.
- Use in the Grok Bot phone app counts too.
- Nomo Tokens and the Activity screen show Grok Bot's real token counts.
- Your own Cursor use, in the Cursor editor or its command line, shows up too,
  on its own line: **Cursor**.
- On The X Subscription, Grok Bot and Cursor share one weekly limit, and Nomo
  shows it. On an older Cursor plan, Grok Bot has its own limit, and Nomo
  doesn't read it.

## Privacy {#privacy}

- Nomo reads only token counts, per hour. It never reads your messages or
  anything you wrote.
- The counts go straight from Cursor's server to your phone. They aren't
  uploaded anywhere.
- Your Cursor sign-in stays in the iPhone keychain and is sent only to Cursor.

To stop, open Cursor in the **Providers** tab, tap **Usage**, and tap
**Disconnect**. This also stops your Cursor usage in Nomo.
