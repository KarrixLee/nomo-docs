Your phone shows **one session at a time**. Not one per computer, not one per
agent — one, device-wide. Everything below is about that one.

## Nothing appears until you say so

By default, **no session appears on your Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.** If
you paired successfully and your island is empty, nothing is broken.

The island sits on your Lock Screen all day. If every session you started
claimed it, one busy afternoon would bury your phone in sessions you stopped
caring about an hour ago. So the app lists everything, and the island shows
only what you chose.

To put a session there, open the computer, find the session, and flip its
switch. To have Nomo do it for you on every new session, turn on **Auto add new
sessions** in [Settings](/sessions/settings#dynamic-island) — a beta setting,
because iOS meters how often the island may repaint and a large batch of live
sessions will bump into that ceiling.

<Note>
  One exception, and it's deliberate: with **Remote approvals** on, a session
  that's **asking permission** appears on the island even with its switch off —
  and its switch shows on and greyed out for as long as the prompt waits. Your
  setting isn't changed. [What that looks
  like](/sessions/troubleshooting).
</Note>

## Which session it picks

When several sessions are eligible, Nomo doesn't show you the newest — it shows
you the one most likely to be blocking you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Anything waiting on you wins">
    A session asking permission, or one that stopped and needs an instruction,
    outranks anything still working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The oldest waiter is sticky">
    Among sessions waiting on you, the one that has been waiting longest holds
    the island. A newer prompt **cannot** shove it aside — otherwise the thing
    you were about to answer would keep getting stolen from under your thumb.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Otherwise, most recent activity">
    Between working sessions, the freshest one shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sessions that have been silent for over half an hour drop out of the running
entirely. If everything eligible has finished and gone quiet, the island clears
itself.

Swapping happens in place — the same card updates, it doesn't flicker away and
come back. There's no way to pin one session there permanently; the rule above
is the whole story.

While a session is working, the status line alternates between `Working` and
the model it's using — `⚡ Fable`, `⚡ GPT 5.6 Sol`. When the agent reports what it's
doing, that rides along too: `Working · Editing`, `Working · Running command`.

## The timer

While a session works, the compact side counts up. For the first five minutes
you get live `M:SS` ticking; after that it settles into `7m`, `1h 2m`, and past
ten hours into `10h`, so the island isn't flickering at you all day.

The clock is anchored to the **current turn**, not the whole session — it
restarts each time you send a new prompt. When the session finishes, the clock
freezes at the time it actually ended, and heartbeats afterwards can't inflate
it.

If Nomo can't work out a plausible start time, it shows no timer rather than a
wrong one.

## When it goes quiet

The island shows the last state your Mac sent. After **10 minutes** of silence
it stops pretending: the card drains of colour, the timer disappears, and the
status becomes `Disconnected?`. Your Mac sleeping, losing Wi-Fi, or the terminal
being closed all land here.

Opening the Nomo app forces a refresh, and a stale state corrects itself
immediately. The plugin also sends a heartbeat every five minutes on its own, so
a genuinely alive but idle session doesn't get marked disconnected.

Finished sessions never go stale — there's nothing left to hear from.

<Tip>
  If island updates feel sluggish, turn on **More Frequent Updates** in iOS
  Settings → Nomo → Live Activities. That's Apple's own budget for how often an
  app may repaint the island, and Nomo is written against the default.
</Tip>
