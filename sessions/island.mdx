---

Once a session is showing on your island, you can read its state without
unlocking your phone. There are only two signals that matter.

{/* TODO: side-by-side screenshot — yellow state vs purple hand state */}

## Yellow means "look at me"

Claude stopped and wants you. It finished a turn, or it needs an instruction
only you can give.

There is nothing to tap. Yellow is a notification, not a question — you have to
go back to your Mac to move it forward.

## The purple hand means "answer me"

Claude is asking permission for a specific action, and **this is the one you can
answer from your phone.** The hand icon is the tell: if you see it, Allow and
Deny buttons are there.

<Tip>
  If you remember one thing from this page: **hand = tappable.** Everything else
  is informational.
</Tip>

Tap into the island and answer it. Claude picks up immediately — see
[Answering from your phone](/sessions/approvals).

## The timer

A live session shows how long it's been running. For the first minute you'll see
seconds ticking; after that it settles into a coarser label so the island isn't
flickering at you all day.

## When the island goes quiet

The island shows the last state your Mac sent. If your Mac sleeps, loses
network, or the session ends abruptly, the island keeps showing the last thing
it heard.

Open the Nomo app to force a refresh. If the state was stale, it corrects
itself immediately.

## Nothing shows up at all

That's the default, and it's deliberate — sessions are opt-in per session. See
[Choosing what appears](/sessions/visibility).
