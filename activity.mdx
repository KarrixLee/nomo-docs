of Nomo.

## Open it

1. Open Nomo.
2. Tap the **Home** tab.
3. Scroll down to **Activity**.

You can also tap the Nomo Activity or Nomo Tokens widget. Nomo opens on Home
at Activity. See [Pro widgets](/widgets#nomo-activity).

To get your latest activity, pull down on Home.

## What it shows

- **Coding now**: while an agent is running, a row at the top says so, such as
  **Claude Code is coding now**, with the computer's name.
- **Your streak**: how many days in a row you've coded with an agent.
- **Today**, **This week** and **Active days**: your coding time today and
  this week, and how many days you coded this week.
- **The calendar**: one square for each of the last 30 days. A stronger color
  means more coding that day. With Nomo Pro, it shows the last 26 weeks.
- **Totals**: your active hours, your active days and your hours per week.
- **Your agents**: how your time splits between agents, such as
  `Claude Code 82%` and `Codex 18%`.

It counts Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Devin and Antigravity on your paired
computers, plus Grok Bot and Cursor. What each one needs is under
[Which agents count](/widgets#which-agents-count).

Activity adds up all your paired computers. See
[More than one computer](/widgets#more-than-one-computer).

Your streak doesn't reset in the morning. Until your first session of the day,
it counts up to yesterday.

## Choose a character {#character}

An animated character sits on the streak card. It follows the agent you use
most. To pick another one:

1. Touch and hold the streak card.
2. The card turns over to **Character**.
3. Tap a character, such as **Clawd**, **Codex** or **Grok Bot**. Or tap
   **Automatic** to follow your top agent again.

The card turns back by itself. To go back without a change, tap **×**.

Your pick also sets Activity's colors. Widgets keep their own **Character**
option in **Edit Widget**.

## Your Wrapped {#wrapped}

Your Wrapped turns your week or month into one image you can share.

1. On Home, scroll to **Your Wrapped**, just under your streak.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Week** (the last 7 days) or **Month** (the last 30 days).
4. Pick a style: bars, lines or heatmap.
5. Tap **Share**, then pick where to send it.

You can share it as a **Post** or a **Story**. The big number is your streak
or your coding time.

With Nomo Pro, you can also pick **Year** (this calendar year so far), and
show your tokens as the big number.

Under Your Wrapped:

1. Tap **7 days**, **14 days** or **30 days** to set the range.
2. Next to **Tokens**, tap **Model** or **Agent** to split each day by model or
   by agent.
3. Tap the line icon for lines, or the bar icon for bars. At first, the chart
   shows lines.

The card shows your total, **Today**, **Daily average**, **Active hours** and
the change from the previous period. By model, the chart shows your top 5
models. The rest are added up as **Other**.

Tokens here means input + output + reasoning. Cache isn't counted: it re-reads
the same context, so it would swamp every other number. The **By model** table
under the chart lists **Input**, **Output**, **Cache** and **Total** for each
model.

Nomo remembers your choices for next time.

### Filter the chart {#tokens-filter}

To hide a model or an agent, tap its name under the chart. Tap it again to
show it. The numbers above the chart still count everything.

Or pick from a list:

1. Touch and hold the Tokens card.
2. The card turns over to **Filter**.
3. Tap a tile to hide it or show it. A checkmark means it's shown.
4. To show everything again, tap **Show all**.
5. To go back, tap **×**.

You can't hide the last one: it shows a lock.

## Privacy

Activity uses only counts: days, active time, sessions and tokens. Never your
prompts, your code, project names or file paths. The full list is under
[Privacy](/widgets#privacy) on the widgets page.
