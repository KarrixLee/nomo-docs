Pairing a computer with Nomo is free. Your streak, your coding time and each
computer's status are free too.

Nomo Pro is the real-time part: your live sessions on the Lock Screen and in
the Dynamic Island, and answering your agents from your phone.

Status, usage and alerts don't need a computer at all. See
[Status and Usage](/status-usage).

## What you get

| | Free | Nomo Pro <span className="pro-pill" /> |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paired computers | One | More |
| Your streak, current and longest. Every agent counts | ✓ | ✓ |
| Activity history (the heatmap) | Last 30 days | All of it |
| Coding time, today and this week | ✓ | ✓ |
| Tokens: totals, charts, by model or by agent | | ✓ |
| Wrapped share card | **Week** and **Month**, as a **Post** (4:5) or a **Story** (9:16). The big number is your streak or your coding time | Adds **Year**, and tokens as the big number |
| Pro widgets | **Nomo Activity**, small size (your streak) | All five, in every size |
| Sessions tab | Your computers, each with a line that says what its agents are doing now | Every live session, one by one |
| Dynamic Island and Lock Screen Live Activity | | ✓ |
| Answer permission prompts and questions, reply, stop a turn, read the full text | | ✓ |
| Cursor and Grok Bot activity, from your Cursor dashboard | | ✓ |

The **Nomo** status widget and the **Nomo Usage** widget are free for everyone.
See [All widgets](/widgets).

<Note>
  A free pairing sends less from your computer than a Pro one. Exactly what it
  sends is in the plugin's README, under
  [What leaves your computer](https://github.com/KarrixLee/nomo#what-leaves-your-computer).
</Note>

## Get the free part

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the plugin">
    Install the Nomo plugin on your computer. See [Install](/setup/install).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pair your computer">
    In Nomo, tap **Connect your Mac** on **Home**, or **+** in **Sessions**.
    See [Pairing](/setup/pairing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Code as usual">
    Use your agent the way you always do. Your streak and coding time show up
    on **Home** in Nomo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a different computer

Nomo Free connects one computer. To switch to another one, forget the old one
first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Forget the old computer">
    In Nomo, open **Sessions**. Touch and hold the computer, then tap
    **Forget This Computer**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Forget This Computer** again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pair the new computer">
    Pair it the same way as the first one. See [Pairing](/setup/pairing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Want more than one computer at a time? Get Nomo Pro.

## Add Nomo Pro

In the Sessions tab, tap one of your computers to see what Nomo Pro adds.

Your computers stay paired. Once you subscribe, live sessions start within
about a minute.
