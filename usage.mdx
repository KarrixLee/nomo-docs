Usage works like [Status](/status) — a widget plus notifications — with one
extra step: it reads numbers off your account, so it needs a sign-in. Still no
Mac plugin; this one is phone-only.

## Which providers

Six: **Claude**, **Codex**, **Cursor**, **xAI**, **Antigravity** and **Kimi** —
everything [Status](/status) covers except DeepSeek, which is status-only.

All six can **alert** you too, at 70%, 90% and 100% of whichever window they
report — see [Usage alerts](/notifications#usage-alerts).

One label differs between the two widgets. Usage lists **Codex**, where Status
watches **OpenAI** as a whole — because the plan you're spending is a Codex
plan, while the thing that goes down is OpenAI.

**Antigravity** reads your **Google Antigravity** quota, including the separate
**Claude & GPT** limit, through Google sign-in rather than a token you paste.

**Kimi** reads your **Kimi Code** plan's 5-hour and weekly limits. Connecting
shows a short code; approve it on Kimi's sign-in page and you're done.

## Signing in

Open the provider from the **Providers** tab and connect the account you
already pay for. Nomo reads the same numbers that account can see about itself
— it isn't an API key and there's nothing to paste.

<Note>
  **Your login stays on your phone.** Tokens are held in the device keychain
  and go only to the provider they belong to — your Claude token to Anthropic,
  your OpenAI token to OpenAI. Usage is fetched on-device and never passes
  through Nomo's servers.
</Note>

Nomo is unofficial and not affiliated with any provider it reads.

### Cursor and Grok {#cursor-and-grok}

Cursor and Grok sign in through Safari, so your passkey, password manager and
existing Safari sign-ins all work.

**Cursor**

1. Tap **Connect**.
2. iOS asks to use **cursor.com** to sign in. Tap **Continue**.
3. Sign in on Cursor's page.

The page closes by itself, and your usage appears. Sign in again about every
60 days.

**Grok**

1. Tap **Connect**.
2. iOS asks to use **x.ai** to sign in. Tap **Continue**.
3. xAI's page opens, and Nomo shows a short code, such as `ABCD-1234`.
4. Check that it matches the code on xAI's page.
5. Approve it on xAI's page.

Nomo renews your Grok sign-in by itself.

Grok usage needs a paid Grok plan, such as SuperGrok or an X subscription. A
free account stays connected, but shows **Grok usage needs a paid Grok plan**
instead of bars.

<Note>
  After you update to Nomo 2.4.0, sign in to Grok once more. Older Grok
  sign-ins don't carry over.
</Note>

## The Pace line <span className="pro-pill" /> {#pace}

With Nomo Pro, each limit on the **Usage** page has one more line. It tells you
if your usage will last until the limit resets.

<Frame caption="Both limits are being used faster than time is passing, so both lines are amber.">
  <div className="device-frame">
    <img className="only-light" src="/images/usage-pace-light.webp" alt="Claude's Your usage card: Session at 82% with the line 82% used · 60% time · Runs out in ~39m, and Weekly at 68% with 68% used · 39% time · Runs out in ~1d 7h, both in amber" />
    <img className="only-dark" src="/images/usage-pace-dark.webp" alt="Claude's Your usage card: Session at 82% with the line 82% used · 60% time · Runs out in ~39m, and Weekly at 68% with 68% used · 39% time · Runs out in ~1d 7h, both in amber" />
  </div>
</Frame>

The line starts with two numbers, such as `45% used · 60% time`:

- **used**: how much of the limit you've used.
- **time**: how much of the time window has gone by.

If used is lower than time, you're fine. After the numbers, you'll see one of
these:

- `Lasts until reset`: you're fine.
- `Runs out in`, then a time, such as `Runs out in ~2d 6h`. It's amber. At your
  current speed, you'd run out before the reset. The `~` means it's an
  estimate.
- `Too early to tell`: the window has only just started.
- `Maxed out`: you've used the whole limit.

Some Cursor plans have no pace. Their limits show no line.

The [Nomo Pace](/widgets#nomo-pace) widget shows the same thing on your Home
Screen.

**Without Nomo Pro**, the first limit shows one row instead: **See if you'll
run out before reset**, with a **PRO** tag. Tap it to open Nomo Pro.

## When you hit a limit

Once any window reaches 100%, a **Limit reached** callout appears under the
usage card with one button — **Manage usage at Claude**, or whichever provider
you're looking at. It opens that provider's own usage settings in your browser,
where extra usage may be available on your plan.

Nomo can't see what's on offer there and sells nothing itself. It's a shortcut
to a page you'd otherwise go looking for.

The callout shows for Claude, Codex, Cursor and xAI, on App Store accounts in
the United States, the European Union and Japan.

## More than one account <span className="pro-pill" /> {#more-than-one-account}

work Claude account and a personal one. Each account gets its own row, with its

### Add another account {#add-another-account}

1. Open Nomo.
2. Tap the **Providers** tab.
3. Tap the provider, such as **Claude**.
4. Tap **Usage** at the top.
5. Make sure you're signed in to your first account.
6. Under your account, tap **Add another account**.
7. Tap **Continue**.
8. Sign in with the other account. Use a different email than your first one.

<Frame caption="Tap Continue to open the sign-in page. The X closes it without adding anything.">

Nomo opens a fresh, private sign-in page, so it doesn't reuse the account
you're already signed in with. The accounts you've already added stay signed
in.

For Claude, Claude shows you a code after you sign in:

1. Copy the code.
2. Go back to Nomo. Nomo pastes the code and connects for you.
3. If nothing happens, tap **Paste**, then tap **Connect**.

A new account is named after its email address, if the provider shares one. If
you sign in to an account that's already in Nomo, you'll see **This account is
already added**, and nothing changes.

When a provider has 5 accounts, **Add another account** is greyed out.

### Rename, Default and Remove {#manage-accounts}

Tap the **···** next to an account, then tap one of these:

- **Rename**: give the account a short name, such as `Work` or `Personal`.
- **Make Default**: make it your Default account. The Default account has a
  **Default** tag.
- **Remove**: sign this account out of Nomo. Your other accounts stay signed
  in. Widgets that showed it show **Account removed** and ask you to pick
  another account. They don't switch to your Default account.

The **Your usage** card at the top shows your Default account. To see another
account there, tap the account name in the card's corner and pick one.

### Accounts in widgets {#accounts-in-widgets}

Widgets show your Default account unless you pick another one:

1. Touch and hold the widget.
2. Tap **Edit Widget**.
3. Tap **Account** and pick one.

**Account** is on [Nomo Usage](/widgets#nomo-usage) and
[Nomo Pace](/widgets#nomo-pace). [Nomo Accounts](/widgets#nomo-accounts) shows
every account at once.

### Without Nomo Pro {#without-nomo-pro}

Without Nomo Pro, you get one account per provider. **Add another account**
shows a **PRO** tag. Tap it to open Nomo Pro. Once you subscribe, Nomo goes
straight on to adding the account. Demo mode doesn't unlock extra accounts.

If your Nomo Pro ends, your extra accounts are paused, not removed. They show
**Paused · PRO**, and only your Default account keeps updating. Your Default
account is never paused. To pick up where you left off, tap **Resubscribe**.

A Nomo Usage widget set to a paused account shows **Paused · Nomo Pro** and
`Your Nomo Pro ended. Resubscribe in Nomo to see this account.` Tap it to open
Nomo Pro.
