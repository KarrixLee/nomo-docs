Usage works like [Status](/status) — a widget plus notifications — with one
extra step: it reads numbers off your account, so it needs a sign-in. Still no
Mac plugin; this one is phone-only.

## Which providers

All five that [Status](/status) covers: **Claude**, **Codex**, **Cursor**,
**xAI** and **Gemini**.

One label differs between the two widgets. Usage lists **Codex**, where Status
watches **OpenAI** as a whole — because the plan you're spending is a Codex
plan, while the thing that goes down is OpenAI.

**Gemini** reads the daily quota on your **Gemini Code Assist** account, through
Google sign-in rather than a token you paste.

## Signing in

Open the provider from the **Providers** tab and connect the account you
already pay for. Nomo reads the same numbers that account can see about itself
— it isn't an API key and there's nothing to paste.

<Note>
  **Your login stays on your phone.** Tokens are held in the device keychain
  and go only to the provider they belong to — your Claude token to Anthropic,
  your OpenAI token to OpenAI. Usage is fetched on-device and never passes
  through Nomo's servers.
</Note>

Nomo is unofficial and not affiliated with any provider it reads.
