<Update label="November 2026" description="2.3.6">
  ## Update reminder and your AI plans

  **New**

  - An update reminder: when a newer Nomo is on the App Store, a small bar says so — above the tab bar on iOS 26.1 and later, or as a card at the top of **Home** on earlier iOS. **Update** goes straight to the App Store. Tap anywhere else on the bar for **What’s New**, with the release notes, **Update** and **Not Now**. Close the bar (×) and it stays hidden until the next version. It shows only once you've finished the welcome guide

  **Improved**

  - The Claude usage card shows your plan — **Claude Pro**, **Claude Max** or **Claude Ultra** — in the same plan line xAI and Antigravity already show. Max 20x shows as Claude Ultra, the name Anthropic gave it on October 30, 2026. The Codex card shows your ChatGPT plan too, such as **ChatGPT Plus** or **ChatGPT Pro**
  - With no 5-hour limit, the medium usage widget shows **Weekly** and your top model's weekly limit, such as Fable, the way it already does for Antigravity

  **Fixed**

  - With the small usage widget set to **Session**, a plan without a 5-hour limit showed an empty number — Claude Ultra, for one, since Anthropic dropped its 5-hour limit. It now shows a checkmark and **No session limit**, as the app already did
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="2.3.5">
  ## Antigravity sessions

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/changelog-2-3-5-antigravity.png" alt="The Nomo and Antigravity icons beside the headline “It asks. You answer.”, and an Antigravity approval card lifting off an iPhone with Deny and Allow buttons" />
  </Frame>

  **New**

  - **Antigravity** joins Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor — sessions from the Google Antigravity desktop app and the `agy` CLI run live in the Sessions tab and on the Dynamic Island, with Antigravity's own conversation title and model. Install plugin 2.3.5 with `bunx nomo-ai --antigravity`, restart Antigravity, then type `/nomo-pair`
  - Allow or deny Antigravity's shell commands and file writes from your phone, with **Deny with note…** to tell it why. Reads and searches never reach your phone, and your own deny rules always win
  - Stop a running Antigravity turn from your phone — **Interrupt** stops it after the current step
  - [Reply](/sessions/antigravity#reply-from-your-phone) to an Antigravity session from your phone — up to 4,000 characters, read at its next step. The reply sheet is a chat: Antigravity's requests appear in it with **Deny** and **Allow**, and [**Deny & send**](/sessions/antigravity#deny-and-send) denies a command and tells Antigravity why in one tap. A finished `agy` session starts again on your Mac; a finished desktop-app session continues only on the Mac. For now, replies need your iPhone and Mac on the same Wi-Fi
  - Cursor's status now covers **BugBot**, **Cursor IDE (iOS)** and **Enterprise Dashboard**, each with its own status alert. If you'd turned off all of Cursor's alerts, the new ones start off too

  **Improved**

  - New buttons across Sessions: **View plan**, **Open on Mac**, **Interrupt** and **Reply** share one look, each in its own color. On question cards, **Deny** is no longer red, matching approval cards
  - When remote approvals are paused, the banner in Sessions lists each agent with the command that turns them back on. Touch and hold a command to copy it
  - The reply box sends what you type exactly as typed: `--` no longer turns into a dash, and quotes stay straight, so commands arrive intact

  **Fixed**

  - Cursor's status history and uptime were empty after Cursor moved its status page. They're back, and uptime now counts a partial outage the way Cursor's own status page does

  **Plugin 2.3.5**

  - Adds Antigravity support: one block named `nomo` in `~/.gemini/config/hooks.json`, leaving your other hooks alone, plus a small Antigravity plugin, `nomo`, with `/nomo-pair`, `/nomo-status`, `/nomo-approvals`, `/nomo-reset` and `/nomo-unpair`. `--uninstall` removes only those two
</Update>

<Update label="October 23, 2026" description="2.3.4">
  ## Security improvements

  **New**

  - Approvals need Face ID: with your iPhone locked, **Allow** on the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island asks for Face ID first. **Deny** stays one tap. It's on by default — **Settings → Sessions → Face ID**
  - **Even When Unlocked** asks for Face ID on every Allow, even with your iPhone unlocked — in the app and on the Dynamic Island. Off by default
  - Turning either switch down asks for Face ID, and turning off **Face ID to Allow** warns you first

  **Improved**

  - You see the real command: a shell command waiting for approval shows on the island as the command itself. A long one is shortened in the middle, so you see how it starts and how it ends, with **+N lines** when there's more. The agent's description of it sits underneath as a caption
  - If your phone can't confirm that an approval card matches the request waiting on your computer, it offers **Deny** only
  - The pairing confirm screen always pairs the computer it shows you
  - **Forget This Computer** keeps trying to cancel the pairing on Nomo's server until it goes through, even if you were offline when you tapped it
  - A new pairing error, **Couldn't Verify This iPhone**, for when Nomo's server can't verify your phone

  **Plugin 2.3.4**

  - Shell approval cards keep the command: the agent's description is kept short, and it's the first thing dropped when space runs out
  - **Always allow** from your phone stays in that session — it's never saved to your project's settings
  - Nomo's hooks no longer load your project's `bunfig.toml` or `.env`
  - `status` now warns if your Bun is affected by CVE-2026-111497 (fixed in Bun 1.4.4) — update Bun if you see it. It also warns when Node.js 22 is older than **22.23.4**
  - OpenCode: `/nomo-update` only ever updates Nomo's own checkout, and `bunx nomo-ai` updates only the global install. Update a project install with `/nomo-update` inside that project
</Update>

<Update label="October 22, 2026" description="2.3.3">
  ## iPhone Duo, Kimi, DeepSeek and Antigravity

  **New**

  - **iPhone Duo** support, and a better iPad layout: the Providers and Sessions tabs show the list and the details side by side
  - **Kimi** and **DeepSeek** join the Status tab, status alerts and the widget
  - **Kimi Code** usage, with the widget and alerts at 70%, 90% and 100%. Connect it from the Providers tab: Nomo shows a short code, and you approve it on Kimi's sign-in page
  - Gemini usage is now **Antigravity**: it reads your Google Antigravity quota, and the provider shows as Antigravity across the app and the widget. The small usage widget can show its **Claude & GPT** limit. If you'd connected Gemini, sign in again
  - Each provider in **Settings → Alerts** has its own switch, to turn all of its status alerts off, or back on, in one go

  **Improved**

  - Usage alert rows carry the provider's icon, like the status rows
  - The welcome guide has a new **Track your usage** page to connect your accounts, and alerts are no longer marked Beta

  **Fixed**

  - On the iPhone Duo's narrow, vertical Dynamic Island, a session's icons sit centered, with a short time label in place of the ticking timer
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="2.3">
  ## Cursor, and Interrupt

  **New**

  - **Cursor** joins Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode — sessions from the Cursor desktop app and `cursor-agent` run live in the Sessions tab and on the Dynamic Island, with Cursor's own chat title and model. Read-only in this version: you watch from your phone and answer at your computer. Install with `bunx nomo-ai --cursor`
  - Choose which agents a computer shows — the **+** button (**Choose agents**) in its drawer picks up to three tabs
  - Stop a running Claude Code or Codex turn from your phone — **Interrupt**, beside **Open on Mac** in a session row. It stops the turn and leaves the session open — Codex straight away, Claude Code at its next tool call
  - Interrupt needs plugin **2.3.0** on your computer — update both the Claude Code and Codex plugins
  - Interrupt needs **Local network** turned on and both devices on the same network: it goes over the direct link, never the relay
  - A widget set to Cursor's **Grok Bot** shows an animated Grok bot in place of the Cursor logo. It follows the status: it dozes at night, looks worried when the service slows down, and turns glum during an outage

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/changelog-2-3-moved-in.webp" alt="A small round bot leaping onto an iPhone Home Screen and landing in a Nomo widget" />
  </Frame>

  **Improved**

  - Pressing Esc in Codex reaches your phone straight away, instead of 20 seconds later
  - The plugin registers three more Codex hooks. After updating, run `/hooks` in Codex and trust the new entries — ten in all
  - Cursor's status on the widget is set a little smaller, so **Major outage** and the other longer states fit better

  **Fixed**

  - A Claude Code turn that ended on an API error read **Running** for up to half an hour. It now settles to **Done**
  - A tool that failed could leave a Claude Code session asking for you after it had moved on
  - A Claude Code session waiting on a background subagent keeps reading as working, instead of **Done** too early
  - A prompt from an OpenCode subagent now shows on its session, and the session goes back to working as soon as it's answered
  - On a slow connection the Dynamic Island could go stale — **Disconnected?** — in the middle of a turn. The plugin now re-sends what didn't get through, and the Sessions tab brings a stale island back once the Mac confirms the session is alive
  - The Status tab, alerts and the widget follow xAI's new status page. A widget set to one of xAI's APIs showed the first API in the list whichever one you picked
</Update>

<Update label="October 10, 2026" description="2.2.2">
  ## More ways to answer

  **New**

  - Approve a Claude Code plan and choose how it carries on — **Approve + auto-accept edits** or **Approve + auto mode** — rather than a plain approval only. Other Claude Code prompts get **Approve + switch to auto mode**
  - Type your own answer to a question with **Other…**, and attach a note to it with **Add note**, instead of only picking from the list
  - Codex questions that offer a free-text answer take **Other…** too
  - An option that comes with a preview shows it under the list when you pick it
  - All of the above need plugin **2.2.2** on your computer — the app keeps them hidden until it's there
  - When a limit reaches 100%, the Usage tab links to that provider's own usage settings — **Manage usage at Claude** — for Claude, Codex, Cursor and xAI, in the US, EU and Japan

  **Improved**

  - On a plan, **Deny with note…** now reads **Keep planning…**, which is what it does

  **Fixed**

  - A Claude Code session opened or resumed and left at an empty prompt read **Running** for up to half an hour. It now settles to **Done** in about 20 seconds
  - OpenCode sessions created without a prompt — a fork, or one opened by another client — no longer sit at **Running**
  - Usage could freeze on an old snapshot and keep presenting it as current
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" description="2.2">
  ## OpenCode

  **New**

  - **OpenCode** joins Claude Code and Codex — its sessions run live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with its own mark, colour and typeface
  - Approve or deny OpenCode's permission requests and its questions from the Lock Screen, the same as the other two. The card tells you what denying will do, because a refusal in OpenCode ends the turn
  - An OpenCode session reads **Planning** while its plan agent is working, and **View plan** opens its todo list
  - **One command** — `bunx nomo-ai` (or `npx nomo-ai`) finds Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode on your computer and installs Nomo into the ones you pick

  **Improved**

  - The paywall names all three agents, and describes approvals for what they are — you answer from the Lock Screen, rather than only being notified
  - A session whose provider is retrying reads **Reconnecting…** instead of a raw error

  **Security**

  - A `nomo://` link can no longer pair a computer on its own — it names the pairing and the server it would connect to, and waits for you to confirm
  - Your subscription is reported to each paired computer separately, so no computer learns about the others
  - Disconnecting Cursor clears its cookies, instead of leaving a session behind that could quietly sign you back in

  **Fixed**

  - The **Local** chip on a paired computer no longer truncates to “L…”
</Update>
