<Update label="August 11, 2026" description="2.1">
  ## Answer your agent from the Lock Screen

  - Permission requests now appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — approve or deny without going back to your desk
  - Current plan and remaining usage visible at a glance on the session
  - **LAN mode** — when your iPhone and computer share a Wi-Fi network, updates travel directly between them, still end-to-end encrypted
  - Grok (xAI) and Gemini (Google) join Claude, OpenAI, and Cursor for status and usage
  - Fixed Japanese and other non-Latin text getting cut off in widgets
  - Layout and stability fixes across widgets, notifications, and Live Activities
</Update>

<Update label="July 14, 2026" description="2.0">
  ## Sessions

  - New **Sessions** tab — pair your computer by QR scan and watch Claude Code and Codex sessions run live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  - Working, done, and needs-you states, plus the active model and session title
  - End-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the key never leaves your devices and the relay only sees ciphertext
  - Usage for Codex and Cursor, alongside Claude
  - Providers hub — status and usage in one place
  - Nomo Pro unlocks live Sessions
  - Demo mode in Settings previews everything, no account needed
  - Now in six languages
</Update>

<Update label="June 19, 2026" description="1.0">
  ## First release

  - Status widget — service health on your Home Screen
  - Usage widget — how much of your plan is left
  - Notifications for both
</Update>
