<Update label="August 19, 2026" description="2.1.1">
  ## Fixes and polish

  **New**

  - Usage alerts now cover **xAI/Grok** and **Gemini**, alongside Claude's per-model weekly cap
  - Read a **full plan** from your phone, instead of only the first part of it

  **Improved**

  - Translations corrected across every language — a number of labels said the wrong thing, or used two different words for the same idea
  - Permission cards lead with what the agent wants to do, rather than the raw shell command
  - Dynamic Island: session titles and the timer no longer get squeezed or cut off
  - iPad: pages are capped to a readable width instead of stretching edge to edge
  - Smaller download

  **Fixed**

  - The paywall showed only free trials when a paid introductory offer was available
  - **Open on Mac** didn't work for Codex sessions
  - Cursor's two usage pools are reachable again on the small widget
  - A crash in the first-launch guide
</Update>

<Update label="August 11, 2026" description="2.1">
  ## Answer your agent from the Lock Screen

  **New**

  - Permission requests appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — approve or deny without going back to your desk
  - Current plan and remaining usage visible at a glance on the session
  - **LAN mode** — when your iPhone and computer share a Wi-Fi network, updates travel directly between them, still end-to-end encrypted
  - Grok (xAI) and Gemini (Google) join Claude, OpenAI, and Cursor

  **Fixed**

  - Japanese and other non-Latin text getting cut off in widgets
  - Layout and stability across widgets, notifications, and Live Activities
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="2.0">
  ## Sessions

  **New**

  - **Sessions** tab — pair your computer by QR scan and watch Claude Code and Codex sessions run live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  - Working, done, and needs-you states, plus the active model and session title
  - End-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the key never leaves your devices and the relay only sees ciphertext
  - Nomo Pro unlocks live Sessions

  **Improved**

  - Usage shows both Used and Left

  **Fixed**

  - OpenAI weekly usage counting
</Update>

<Update label="June 19, 2026" description="1.0">
  ## First release

  - Status widget — service health on your Home Screen
  - Usage widget — how much of your plan is left
  - Notifications for both
</Update>
